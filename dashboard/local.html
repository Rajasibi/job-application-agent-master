Visible search

Opens a real browser window on this computer with your saved login and reads the job results slowly (5 s between pages). If a CAPTCHA, human check or login appears, the window waits up to 5 minutes for you to solve it, then continues. "Access Denied" stops the run. It never tries to get around a check.

Paste a job

Paste a Naukri, foundit, Indeed or company careers link. It goes through the normal pipeline: duplicate check, fit score (70+), tailored CV and cover letter, then auto-apply (Naukri / foundit, 80+) or preparation for your review. For Indeed, also paste the job description.
